# Repository maintenance

- `scoville-ui-anti-ai-slop/` is the independently installable package.
- `docs/` contains maintained documentation, provenance and evidence.
- `tests/` contains development tests or case definitions. Case definitions
  alone do not prove model behavior.
- `benchmarks/` retains frozen versioned fixtures at their recorded paths.
  They are development evidence, not part of the installed package.
